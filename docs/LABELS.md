# Labels

Use labels to communicate domain, risk, and workflow state; labels do not replace review requirements.

Recommended domains: `area:api`, `area:market-data`, `area:strategy`, `area:risk`, `area:execution`, `area:broker`, `area:portfolio`, `area:tfex`, `area:security`, `area:database`, `area:observability`, `area:docs`, `area:ci`.

Recommended types: `type:bug`, `type:feature`, `type:docs`, `type:security`, `type:incident`, `type:maintenance`.

Recommended risk: `risk:low`, `risk:medium`, `risk:high`, `risk:money-moving`.

Recommended state: `status:needs-triage`, `status:blocked-external`, `status:needs-uat`, `status:needs-evidence`.

The labeler workflow may apply safe path-based labels automatically; humans remain responsible for risk classification.
