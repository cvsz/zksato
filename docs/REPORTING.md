# Reporting

## Operational reports
Daily account/portfolio snapshot, realized/unrealized P&L, orders/fills/rejects, strategy activity, risk rejections, drawdown/exposure, broker/feed incidents, reconciliation differences.

## Research reports
Backtest metadata/results, parameter/version, sample windows, fees/slippage, walk-forward results, paper/UAT promotion evidence.

## Audit exports
Authorized export only, redacted as required, stable correlation IDs, immutable source records, reproducible generation version.
