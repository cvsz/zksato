# Code of Conduct

## Our standard
Contributors, reviewers, maintainers, operators, and users are expected to collaborate professionally, respectfully, and with technical rigor.

Constructive behavior includes:
- focusing disagreements on evidence, reproducibility, documented requirements, and trade-offs;
- assuming good faith while still demanding verification for safety-critical claims;
- protecting private/security-sensitive information;
- giving actionable review feedback and acknowledging uncertainty;
- respecting different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized or abusive conduct, deliberate disclosure of private credentials/account data, intimidation, spam, or repeated disruption of technical collaboration.

## Safety-sensitive disagreement
For trading, security, incident, or production-readiness questions, correctness and evidence take precedence over speed or consensus. It is acceptable to block a change when required safety evidence is missing. Do not pressure contributors to bypass `RiskEngine`, reconciliation, authorization, CI, UAT, or documented external gates.

## Security disclosure
Potential vulnerabilities and credentials must follow `SECURITY.md`; do not disclose exploitable details in ordinary issues, PRs, or discussions.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, reject, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented when practical, and focused on protecting participants and project integrity.

## Scope
This policy applies to repository collaboration and project-related communication spaces controlled by maintainers.
