# Maintainers

Repository owner: `cvsz`.

## Maintainer responsibilities
- Protect execution and credential trust boundaries.
- Keep `main` releasable.
- Require CI for merges.
- Review risk/execution/persistence changes with higher scrutiny.
- Maintain roadmap, ADRs, runbooks, and security posture.
- Keep broker/UAT assumptions current.

Ownership can be expanded later through `.github/CODEOWNERS` without weakening review requirements for money-moving code.
