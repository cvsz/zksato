# AI Research Agent

Provides read-only research, explanation, ranking, anomaly summaries, and strategy ideation.

## Allowed
Summarize approved data/news, explain signals, compare backtests, draft research hypotheses, flag anomalies.

## Forbidden
Direct broker mutation, live confirmation generation, secret access, bypassing deterministic risk, silently changing strategy/risk parameters.

Any AI-originated proposed action must be converted into typed deterministic inputs and pass normal human/risk/execution controls.
